Critical Credit Card EMI Rules & Hidden Factors

1. Pre-closure Charges

Foreclosing your EMI early usually incurs a **2% to 3% penalty** on the remaining principal balance, plus 18% GST. Minimum lock-in periods may apply.

2. Non-Refundable Fees

The initial processing fee and its 18% GST are **strictly non-refundable**, even if you cancel or pre-close the EMI layout in month one.

3. Credit Limit Blocking

Your credit limit is blocked by the total purchase principal amount. It is only restored incrementally month-by-month as you clear your EMIs.

4. First-Month Bill Shock

Your first statement after conversion will combine the processing fee, GST, and the first month's regular installment, making it noticeably higher.

5. Loss of Reward Points

Standard bank conversion EMI transactions generally **forfeit regular reward points**, milestone benefits, and card-specific cashbacks.

6. No-Cost EMI Realities

True "No-Cost EMIs" discount the interest upfront via merchants. If not merchant-backed, banks may quietly absorb costs through higher processing fees.
